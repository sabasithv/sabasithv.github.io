RESEARCH / THEMES

Functional materials.
Measurable impact.

Exploring the connection between materials science, energy harvesting, sustainable technologies and biomedical applications.

01 / DOMAIN

Nanogenerators & Mechanical Energy Harvesting

Design and performance assessment of triboelectric (TENG), piezoelectric (PENG) and hybrid devices for converting low-frequency mechanical energy into electricity.

Mechanical-to-electrical conversion · device engineering · hybrid generators
02 / DOMAIN

Waste Upcycling & Sustainable Electronics

Transforming challenging waste streams, including discarded clinical and consumer materials, into functional triboelectric layers and self-powered applications.

Waste-to-energy · polymer reuse · sustainable design
03 / DOMAIN

Smart, Ferroelectric & Magnetoelectric Materials

Engineering multifunctional polymer composites, lead-free perovskites and magnetoelectric systems toward flexible energy harvesters, sensors and other functional platforms.

Lead-free perovskites · magnetoelectric coupling · flexible composites
04 / DOMAIN

Biomedical Energy Harvesting & Therapy

Combining hybrid nanogenerators with therapeutic hydrogels to explore electrically stimulated drug release, angiogenesis and wound healing in experimental models.

Bioelectronics · controlled release · wound-healing research
05 / DOMAIN

Self-Powered Sensors & IoT Devices

Translating triboelectric output into meaningful sensing functions such as touch interfaces, environmental monitoring, smart dispensers and event detection.

Human–machine interfaces · sensing · energy-autonomous devices
