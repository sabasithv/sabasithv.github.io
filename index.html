Research, teaching and innovation in one journey.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at IIT Madras working at the intersection of functional materials, nanogenerators and biomedical engineering. My research builds on doctoral work in sustainable triboelectric technologies at VIT Vellore and an earlier academic career in electronics and mechatronics.
